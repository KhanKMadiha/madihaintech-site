My story

Building better systems for support

I started in customer support. Curiosity took me into technical support, and technical depth led me to Support Operations. Today I am most interested in the systems behind support and what happens to everything we learn from customers.

About me

I am a Senior Support Professional working at the intersection of Support, Product and Engineering. At Stack Overflow, I own the EMEA support lifecycle across 100+ enterprise accounts, including complex technical investigations and P1 and P2 escalations.

Over time, I became just as interested in what happens after a ticket is solved. How does a recurring issue become better documentation? How does customer feedback reach Product? How do we give Engineering better evidence when something needs escalating?

That is the direction of my work now: building Support Operations systems that turn what Support learns into something the wider company can use.

What I am building toward

I keep coming back to one question: what happens to the information Support learns?

My projects explore different parts of that loop. Feedback Loop turns customer requests into structured product signals. Support Ticket Analyser turns solved issues into reusable knowledge. My Engineering Escalation Engine explores how customer problems can become clearer, evidence-based engineering handoffs.

I am particularly interested in AI-native and developer-focused teams where Support sits close to Product and Engineering, and where there is room to question how things work and build something better.

Beyond work

Ramadan fundraising: £50,000 raised, 2,000 families reached

Every Ramadan, I lead a fundraising project providing food bags to families in Pakistan. It started small and grew because I kept improving how we did it, from outreach to logistics and follow-through. I think that says a lot about how I approach things generally: if something matters, I want to make it work better.
