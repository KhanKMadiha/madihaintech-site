About me

Curiosity took me beyond solving tickets.

I started in customer support, where my curiosity about the technical side of solving problems led me into enterprise technical support. I now own EMEA escalations at Stack Overflow, working closely with Engineering and Product.

I also taught myself to code and became interested in using AI to build tools inspired by challenges I’d encountered in Support. What began as solving problems in my own workflow grew into finding ways to help other teams work better.

principles.md

// my principles

Investigate thoroughly, provide clear reproduction steps and customer impact, and make sure every escalation has an owner and next steps.

Build practical tools that reduce repetitive work while keeping human judgement where it matters.

Create useful, findable knowledge so customers and internal teams can get answers without unnecessary effort.

Identify patterns in customer feedback and translate them into improvements Product and Engineering can act on.

Set realistic expectations and earlier internal deadlines. If I promise an update tomorrow, I aim to deliver it today.

How I handle incidents and escalations

Beyond solving tickets

As I got deeper into technical Support, I started noticing the same kinds of problems coming up again and again.

I became interested in what happened after an issue was resolved. Could better documentation prevent another ticket? Could we make Engineering escalations easier? How could customer feedback lead to meaningful improvements?

That curiosity led me to teach myself to code, so I could start building the tools I wished existed in Support.

What that curiosity led me to build

  • Feedback LoopTurns recurring customer issues into prioritised Product insights.
  • Engineering Escalation EngineStructures technical escalations and Engineering handoffs using defined rules and human approval.
  • Support Ticket AnalyserIdentifies documentation gaps and opportunities to create reusable knowledge.
