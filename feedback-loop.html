The challenge and what I built

The challenge

One thing I’ve noticed working in Support is how much valuable customer feedback can get lost once a ticket is closed. Customers share frustrations, ideas and feature requests, but those insights don’t always make it back to the teams building the product. I wanted to change that.

What I built

Feedback Loop, an AI-assisted workflow that brings customer requests together, identifies recurring themes, preserves the supporting evidence and generates prioritised Product briefs.

Why I designed it this way

Three decisions shaped how I built Feedback Loop.

  • Keep customer evidence visible

    Product teams can trace every insight back to the original customer requests, rather than relying on an AI-generated summary alone.

  • Prioritise beyond request volume

    I included account context and customer impact so frequent requests don’t automatically outweigh more significant needs.

  • Fit existing Product workflows

    I designed the output as a shareable Product brief so teams can act on the insights without needing to adopt another tool.
