Enterprise Incident & Escalation Management

From customer impact to coordinated resolution.

I own EMEA technical support across 100+ enterprise accounts at Stack Overflow, coordinating incidents and improving escalation processes across Support and Engineering.

What I’ve owned

  • Incident & escalation coordination

    Coordinated enterprise incidents and Engineering escalations, including serving as Incident Commander. Assessed customer impact, brought the right teams together and kept everyone updated through resolution.

  • PagerDuty-to-Rootly migration

    Led the move from PagerDuty to Rootly and created guidance to help the team manage incidents using the new tooling.

  • EMEA escalation processes

    Developed EMEA escalation processes and on-call guidance to make it easier to route incidents to the right Engineering teams.

Incident leadership in practice

Production access incident

Situation
A customer submitted a support ticket reporting a production access failure. I reproduced the issue and identified evidence that the impact was wider than initially reported.
My decisions & actions
I reproduced the issue, initiated the incident and Engineering escalation, reassessed its severity when I identified wider impact and coordinated the response with the relevant teams.
Outcome
Engineering deployed a fix, and I followed up with the customer to confirm that it was live and verify that they could access the platform without further issues.
Reopening an investigation
The situation
A customer reported symptoms matching an issue that had previously been addressed.
My actions
I recognised the link to the earlier investigation, reopened it with updated evidence and brought it back to the incident channel, keeping Engineering and the customer updated throughout.
Outcome
Engineering made further changes, and the customer confirmed that performance was acceptable.

How I approach incidents

Select a step to explore how I approach incidents.

Step 1 of 5

Identify & assess

Validate customer reports, reproduce the issue where possible and establish the scope of impact.

Step 2 of 5

Initiate & escalate

Open the right incident or Engineering escalation, attach the evidence and reassess severity as the picture changes.

Step 3 of 5

Coordinate

Bring the right teams together, keep communications moving, clarify ownership of next steps and follow up on blockers.

Step 4 of 5

Validate recovery

Track the investigation, share technical findings with stakeholders and confirm recovery with customers wherever possible.

Step 5 of 5

Improve the process

Capture what slowed the response and turn it into reusable guidance for the next incident.
