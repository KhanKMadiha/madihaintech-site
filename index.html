What I do

I sit between Support, Product, Engineering and AI.

My work starts with customer problems and ends with systems that make those problems easier to solve at scale. I investigate technical issues, identify recurring patterns, improve support workflows and build AI-assisted operational tools that turn operational knowledge into something the wider organisation can use.

Product Operations & Customer Signals
